Your Guide to Australian Casino Hotel Escapes

Sometimes the best weekend away does not require a complicated itinerary.

A beautiful hotel, an excellent dinner, somewhere lively to spend the evening and a comfortable room waiting upstairs can be enough.

Across Australia, several casino destinations bring those elements together while giving visitors very different surroundings to explore.

From Perth's Swan River to central Brisbane, Adelaide's Riverbank precinct and the landscapes of Tasmania, these properties offer five distinct ways to turn an evening out into a short escape.

Destination 01

Crown Perth

Perth, Western Australia

Resort Style Beside the Swan River

Crown Perth is built for visitors who like having choices.

Three hotels sit within the complex: Crown Towers, Crown Metropol and Crown Promenade. Together they offer different styles of accommodation within the same riverside destination.

The precinct also includes a spa, resort-style pools, a theatre and a wide collection of restaurants and bars.

That variety means a stay can change character throughout the day.

Spend the afternoon by the pool, dress for dinner, visit the casino later in the evening and return to your hotel without leaving the resort.

Its position beside the Swan River and only minutes from Perth CBD also makes it easy to explore more of the city.

Why choose Crown Perth?Choose it when you want the scale of a large resort without giving up access to Perth.
Destination 02

The Star Brisbane

Brisbane, Queensland

A New-Generation Riverfront Destination

The Star Brisbane has given Queensland's capital a completely new entertainment precinct.

Opened as part of Queen's Wharf Brisbane, the resort combines The Star Grand hotel with restaurants, bars, event spaces and the striking Sky Deck overlooking the Brisbane River.

The five-star Star Grand features contemporary accommodation at the centre of the precinct, while the broader destination extends across the riverfront.

What makes this property interesting is how naturally it connects with Brisbane itself.

Visitors can explore the CBD and South Bank during the day before returning for dinner and an evening at the resort.

The rooftop Sky Deck adds another reason to visit even before the casino enters the picture.

Why choose The Star Brisbane?Choose it for a contemporary city escape with restaurants, river views and Brisbane immediately outside.
Destination 03

Eos by SkyCity

Adelaide, South Australia

A Refined Stay in the Heart of Adelaide

Eos by SkyCity takes a noticeably more intimate approach.

The luxury hotel forms part of the SkyCity Adelaide precinct beside the River Torrens, putting guests close to Adelaide Oval, the Festival Centre, Rundle Mall and many of the city's central attractions.

Accommodation is complemented by spa and wellness facilities, a heated outdoor pool, sauna, steam room and views across the river.

Across the wider SkyCity precinct, guests can choose between multiple restaurants and bars before visiting the casino or heading into the city.

Adelaide also makes an appealing starting point for travellers interested in South Australia's food and wine regions.

Why choose Eos?Choose it when luxury accommodation, food and a central city location matter just as much as the casino.
Destination 04

Wrest Point

Hobart, Tasmania

An Australian Original With Waterfront Views

Wrest Point holds a distinctive place in Australia's casino history, but its location is just as important as its heritage.

Set on the River Derwent in Sandy Bay, the hotel is only a short distance from central Hobart and Salamanca.

Rooms in the tower offer views towards the river, Hobart and kunanyi / Mount Wellington.

Restaurants, bars, entertainment and the casino are all located within the complex, making it easy to spend an entire evening on site.

But Tasmania is likely to tempt visitors outside.

Markets, waterfront dining, galleries, mountain scenery and day trips can all become part of the same stay.

Why choose Wrest Point?Choose it for waterfront surroundings and easy access to Hobart.
Destination 05

Country Club Tasmania

Launceston, Tasmania

Royal Reels Casino, Golf and Space to Slow Down

Country Club Tasmania feels less like an urban hotel and more like a traditional resort getaway.

Located near Launceston, it offers both resort rooms and self-contained villa accommodation, alongside restaurants, bars, leisure facilities and Tasmania's only casino in the Launceston area.

Golf plays a major role here.

The resort has a championship course as well as additional outdoor and leisure activities, giving visitors plenty to do during daylight hours.

That makes Country Club particularly suitable for travellers who want the casino to be only one part of the weekend.

Why choose Country Club Tasmania?Choose it when you want more space, golf and a quieter resort atmosphere.
01

Choose the Weekend You Want

Not every casino escape needs to look the same.

For a large destination resort, Crown Perth offers an extensive selection of hotels, restaurants and facilities in one location.

The Star Brisbane is better suited to travellers who want something contemporary and central.

Eos brings a more refined feel to Adelaide, while Wrest Point combines Hobart sightseeing with an established waterfront hotel.

Country Club Tasmania is the outlier — and that is exactly its appeal. It gives visitors room to slow down and combine casino entertainment with golf and resort activities.

02

One Night or an Entire Weekend?

Before booking, think about how much of your trip you want to spend inside the resort.

A one-night city stay may favour Brisbane or Adelaide, where restaurants and attractions sit close by.

If you prefer a self-contained destination, Crown Perth or Country Club Tasmania gives you more reasons to remain on site.

Tasmania also works particularly well when the resort forms one stop on a longer road trip.

There is no need to choose solely by the size of the casino.

Accommodation, surroundings and what you want to do the next morning are often more important.

03

Turn an Evening Out Into an Escape

The appeal of a casino hotel is simple: the evening does not have to end when dinner or entertainment does.

Stay overnight, enjoy a relaxed breakfast and discover somewhere new the following day.

Australia offers destinations that suit very different travellers, from polished city hotels to spacious Tasmanian resorts.

The best choice is the one that fits naturally into the kind of weekend you already enjoy.

04

Play Responsibly

Casino participation in Australia is restricted to adults aged 18 and over.

Set clear limits before playing and spend only what you are comfortable losing. Do not chase losses or treat casino activities as a source of income.

Support is available through Gambling Help Online and the national helpline at 1800 858 858.
